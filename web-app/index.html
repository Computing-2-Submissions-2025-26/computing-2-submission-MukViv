Chess Thieves

SOUND ON! A thief has stolen a set of chess pieces and is trying to escape. The king needs to catch them before they reach the exit.

Thief

Reach the EXIT square within 15 turns to win. Roll the dice to move and don't get caught!

King

Catch the THIEF to win. Place police cars to block their path (max 5). You also win if the Thief has not escaped after 15 turns.
