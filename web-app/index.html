Chess Thieves

The THIEF tries to reach the EXIT square. The KING blocks the way and tries to catch the thief.

Player 1: Thief

Reach the EXIT square before the King catches you.

  • Pawn (but multidirectional): 1 square up, down, left, or right.
  • Knight: L-shaped chess knight move.
  • Bishop, rook, queen: up to 4 squares.

Royal Board

Roll the dice thief! Press [R] or click the dice on the bottom right!

Game Status

  • Use Arrow keys or WASD to move the cursor.
  • Use Enter or Space to select a square.
  • Press R to roll during Player 1's roll phase.
  • The King wins after 25 thief turns if the Thief has not escaped.
