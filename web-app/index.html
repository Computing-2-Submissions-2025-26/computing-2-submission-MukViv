Chess Thieves

Player 1 steals the pieces. Player 2 guards the royal board.

Player 1: Thief

Reach the EXIT square before the King catches you.

  • Sneak: 1 square up, down, left, or right.
  • Knight: L-shaped chess knight move.
  • Bishop, rook, queen: up to 4 squares.

Royal Board

The Thief rolls first.

Game Status

  • Use Arrow keys or WASD to move the cursor.
  • Use Enter or Space to select a square.
  • The King wins after 25 thief turns if the Thief has not escaped.
